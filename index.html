Customer Portal Homepage · Option C

Simplified Tiles

Built on Option B and revised with Scott’s and Ken’s feedback: fewer concepts, one clear job for each section, and a layout designed for phones first.

Where Things Go

  • To DoThings that need your action. Nothing else.
  • UpdatesThings that happened. No action needed. The bell is the same list.
  • MailYour mail and the status of your requests (scan, forward, shred).
  • MessagesConversations with your mail center or our support team.
  • DocumentsFiles you keep: saved scans and your own uploads.

What Changed From Option B

  • Requests merged into Mail. Request status lives on each mail item.
  • “Needs Your Attention” is now To Do, with action items only.
  • Notifications and the bell are one Updates list.
  • Banner removed. New features get a “New” label instead.
  • Filing Cabinet is now Documents, with tags and search instead of folders. Upload lives there.
  • Each product card is one link, with one status line.
  • Mobile-first layout. Use the Desktop / Mobile switch at the top.

Use ← → to move between steps and Esc to explore on your own. All names and numbers are sample data.
